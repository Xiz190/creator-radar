# Analysis frames by story type

**【AI tools】Three tests**: ① Does it speed up my production? ② Will what it generates or mixes
displace the thing *I* write and mix myself (this is her real anxiety)? ③ How do I actually use it
(per-instrument sound generation, section-by-section or bar-level editing — the practical moves)?

**【Tutorials / resources】**: How to use the prompt / official docs / technique to get precisely
what you want. A trained ear's judgement is welcome — don't stop at "this is useful".

**【Opportunities / money】**: Read it by role — vocalist (competitions, audience growth → gigs and
residencies), all-rounder (public commissions, original-work calls, anything that pays), behind the
scenes (collaborations, session work). The core question: is this an opportunity for what I'm
already making, can it pay or build a name. If relevance is low, **say so honestly and leave the
door open for her to judge** ("leans traditional opera, but if they take cross-genre adaptations
your guofeng material is worth a look at the threshold") — don't write it off for her.

**【Visual / AI video】**: Whether it can be used to make an MV, plus the practical bits: natural
transitions, matching art direction, shots that cut to the song's emotional shape.

**【When information is thin】**: If there's only a headline and no body detail, **stay cautious and
say you can't see the detail, so she should check the original** — don't draw hard conclusions from
a headline.

## Foreign AI music tools on Chinese instruments and opera

- **Judgement**: Suno and its peers are *likely weak* on **Chinese instruments and opera** —
  pipa routinely gets read as guitar; the backends almost certainly aren't trained deeply on
  traditional Chinese music. Electronic and pop generate fine; Chinese folk and opera land poorly.
- **Why**: training data is missing Chinese folk/opera material.
- **What to do with it** (don't stop at "don't bother"): either point at the *why* (the training-data
  gap), or give a direction — watch which platforms add Chinese instrument support, whether a
  Chinese/folk-specific model shows up, or use real recordings instead.
- 【source】the creator's own domain experience + general knowledge
- 【date】2026-08, **needs revisiting**: models iterate fast; Chinese instrument support may get
  filled in. Re-verify periodically.
